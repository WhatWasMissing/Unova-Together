# Optional battle scenery

Battle scenery can show an arena inspired by the area where a battle starts. Turn on **Area battle scenery** in launcher settings before starting the game. The scenery is made from game data on your computer and does not edit your ROM or save. Turn the option off at any time to use the original backgrounds.

The scenes are stylized arenas rather than exact map recreations. Some special cinematic battles keep their original backgrounds, and scenery details vary between areas.

The area is selected from the loaded field zone used by the battle. Nearby terrain is used only when the outdoor matrix cell resolves to that same area; if it does not, the matching area arena is used instead. If the zone data cannot be validated, the original background is restored. The local scene generator keeps props outside a protected center so battlers and battle UI stay visible. The selected scenery remains fixed for the duration of the battle.
