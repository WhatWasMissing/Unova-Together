# Cynthia’s Route 7 challenge

After you defeat Skyla, look for Cynthia near the path between Mistralton City and Route 7. She explains Mega Evolution and gives you one Mega Stone that matches a Pokémon in your party. If your party has no Pokémon that can Mega Evolve yet, she gives you Serperiorite instead. If your Bag is full, make room and speak to her again.

After you receive the stone, Cynthia moves to the northern end of Route 7 and waits for a rematch. Her team is based on her Pokémon Black 2 team and is around the same level as Redux Skyla's team. Her level 52 Garchomp holds Garchompite and can Mega Evolve. You can also find Cynthia in the Practice battle list.

On the reviewed English Black 2 Redux Complete 1.4.1 ROM and the matching Unova Together 1.0.35 ROM, the launcher creates a separate event-ready game copy the first time you press Play. Your selected ROM and save files stay in place. The event is skipped for other game revisions so the launcher can start them normally.
