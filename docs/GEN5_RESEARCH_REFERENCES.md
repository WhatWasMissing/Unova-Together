# Generation 5 research references

Project Pokémon's [Generation 5 technical documentation](https://projectpokemon.org/home/docs/gen-5/) contains the references below. This index preserves their titles, authors, and original links. The project owner also supplied additional file-system, Pokémon-record, PID, and save-layout notes, now organized in the [local Generation 5 reference archive](references/gen5/README.md). The archive identifies the original authors and links, distinguishes Black/White material from Black 2/White 2, and marks unverified addresses and anomalies. Do not treat historical vanilla-game notes as confirmed Redux offsets.
