# View IVs and EVs

Open **Unova → Pokémon → IVs and EVs**, then choose a Pokémon to see its individual and effort values. These read-only values help you understand how your Pokémon’s stats have developed.

Use the controller’s directional buttons and A, or tap a Pokémon on the touch screen. This view does not change the Pokémon or its save data.
