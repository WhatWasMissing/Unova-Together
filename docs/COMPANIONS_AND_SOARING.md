# Companion interactions

Enable **Companion interactions** in **Launcher → Settings → Experimental**, then restart the game. Open **Unova → Pokémon → My companion**, or face your follower in the field and press A while standing still. Clicking the follower also opens its interactions. Keep followers enabled in **Unova → Settings**.

Talk, pet and play responses reflect the lead Pokémon's health and friendship. Eggs cannot interact. These interactions do not change friendship, HP, items or save data. Changing the lead Pokémon clears the previous reaction. **Greet teammate’s Pokémon** requires player sharing and a nearby teammate. B returns to the Pokémon tab.

The former Latias/Latios flight preview is removed. Native Fly remains available through the original Pokémon party menu, with the HM and normal story requirements.
