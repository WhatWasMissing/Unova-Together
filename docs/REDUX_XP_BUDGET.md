# Plan experience in Redux Challenge mode

The launcher’s experience calculator gives a rough idea of how solo experience affects progress through Blaze Black 2 Redux Challenge mode. The result is an estimate, not a promise of the level your team will reach: your battles, Pokémon, and starting experience all affect the outcome.

The supported solo bonus is **1.25×**. Co-op battles use the game's normal experience rewards by default; the launcher has an optional EXP-suppression switch for co-op comparison tests. For the full list of game features, see [Features](FEATURES.md).
