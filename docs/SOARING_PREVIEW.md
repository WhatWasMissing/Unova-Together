# Soaring preview

Soaring is an optional, unfinished way to explore 69 outdoor areas in Blaze Black 2 Redux. The routes connect the outdoor locations, but some positions are approximate and landing is available only where there is clear ground.

Enable **Experimental features → Soaring flight** in launcher settings. In the game, open **Unova → Pokémon → Take flight** and follow the on-screen controls. Choose a clear landing spot or use the return option to go back to your take-off point.

Marine Tube has no safe landing spot, so return to your take-off point there. Soaring is separate from the normal Fly move and does not change story progress. For regular travel, use the game's party menu.
