# Known limits

Unova Together adds many features to Blaze Black 2 Redux, and some optional modes have limits to keep in mind.

## Co-op

- Use the same Unova Together release and matching game version on every computer.
- Turn fast-forward off before connecting and during shared battles.
- Co-op experience and level-up handling are experimental. The game uses its normal reward flow by default; **Suppress EXP in co-op battles** in launcher Settings can be enabled to compare behavior. Use the same setting on both computers. Story progress and saves remain separate.
- If a connection drops during a battle, the fight may not resume. Return to the field and start a new session before continuing.
- Some VPNs, firewalls, and restricted networks can block connections. The optional UDP connection needs a server supplied by your group; Unova Together does not provide a public server.
- A Pokémon normally used for a one-on-one trainer battle may be duplicated for the second player.

For connection steps, see the [player guide](PLAYER_GUIDE.md) and [Tailscale guide](TAILSCALE_COOP.md).

## Optional features

- **Battle scenery is optional.** Some special battles keep their original backgrounds, and scenery may vary between areas. Turn it off in launcher settings if you prefer the original look.
- **Companion interactions are cosmetic.** They do not change friendship, HP, items, or save data.
- **Encounter search covers ordinary encounters.** Gifts, scripted legendary Pokémon, and Hidden Grottos are not included.
- **Field HMs keep their normal story requirements.** Fly still follows the game’s party and destination rules.

## Saves and updates

Keep a normal in-game save before installing an update. The launcher keeps your save files when it updates the game. A disconnected battle or interrupted trade may need you to return to the field before you continue.
