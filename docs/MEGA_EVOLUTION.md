# Mega Evolution

Unova Together **2.0.17** includes **49 Mega forms** for supported English Blaze Black 2 Redux Complete v1.4.1 games. Every Poké Mart stocks all Mega Stones for **₽50,000** each, and the two Battle Point counters sell the same selection for **50 BP**. Give a Pokémon its matching stone, then hold **L + R together** while choosing a move in battle. Mega Evolution also works in double battles if the other Pokémon chooses its move afterward. The final three Gym Leaders and Cynthia’s Route 7 rematch have Pokémon holding Mega Stones. The player’s Pokémon returns to normal when the battle ends.

You can also receive a free Mega Stone from Cynthia after defeating Skyla. Find her near the Mistralton City and Route 7 transition. If no Pokémon in your party can Mega Evolve, she gives you Serperiorite. Later, challenge her on the northern end of Route 7; her Garchomp holds Garchompite. Read the [Cynthia Route 7 guide](CYNTHIA_ROUTE_7.md) for game-version support details.
