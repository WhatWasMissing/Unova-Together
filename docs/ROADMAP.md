# What may come next

This page describes ideas being explored for future versions. These are possibilities, not promises or features included in the current download.

Unova Together currently includes co-op exploration, supported trainer battles, Pokémon trading, followers, Pokémon tools, Mega Evolution, and launcher tools. See [Features](FEATURES.md) for what is available now.

Ideas that may be considered include clearer connection guidance, more follower reactions and display options, improvements to optional field and battle scenery, and additional accessibility settings.
