# Co-op tools

Open **Unova** from the game’s pause menu and choose the **Co-op** tab. Choose a teammate on the **Players** page before selecting an action that involves them.

Invite a friend to supported trainer battles, choose solo or co-op trainer battles, and browse practice battles that do not award story progress. In story battles, each player’s game remains responsible for that player’s results and rewards.

Use **Players → Give item** to exchange an item, and **Players → Pokémon trade** to trade Pokémon. Use **Pokémon** for party tools and **Settings** to control followers and other options. Switch pages with L/R or touch a tab; press B to close the menu. The game’s normal **Options** entry stays available from its pause menu.

For connection help, see [Playing with friends](PLAYER_GUIDE.md). Some special battles and unstable connections may not work as expected; see [Known limits](KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

The launcher checks that the presence relay matches the current release and supports Teleport. When an outdated relay is running from this installation, **Check relay** or **Host presence relay here** replaces it. For a relay hosted on another computer, update and restart it on the host before reconnecting.
