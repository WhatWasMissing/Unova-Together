# Solo battle experience

On supported English Pokémon Black 2 and Redux profiles, solo battles award **1.25×** the normal experience. No setting is needed. This affects new experience from solo battles; experience your Pokémon already has stays the same.

Co-op battles do not award experience. The launcher’s experience calculator uses the same solo bonus for its estimates. Your actual levels depend on which battles you choose and which Pokémon earn experience.
