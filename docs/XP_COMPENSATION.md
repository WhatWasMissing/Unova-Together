# Solo battle experience

On supported English Blaze Black 2 Redux profiles, solo battles award **1.25×** the normal experience. No setting is needed. This affects new experience from solo battles; experience your Pokémon already has stays the same.

Co-op battles suppress experience, level-up and move-learning rewards by default while this flow is tested. For comparison tests, turn off **Suppress EXP in co-op battles** in launcher Settings on both computers. The launcher’s experience calculator uses the solo bonus for its estimates. Your actual levels depend on which battles you choose and which Pokémon earn experience.
