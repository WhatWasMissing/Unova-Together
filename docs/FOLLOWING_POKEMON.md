# Following Pokémon

The first Pokémon in your party follows about one tile behind. Change the first party slot to choose a different follower. Eggs and fainted Pokémon stay hidden.

Followers follow your turns rather than cutting across corners. They stop when you stop, hide during battles and full-screen menus, and reappear after you return to the field. They are for appearance only: they do not block movement, affect encounters, change your party, or alter your save.

All 649 base species have directional follower artwork. Shiny colors and selected gender or alternate-form artwork are available where matching images were supplied. When a matching alternate-form image is not available, the base artwork is used. Followers can be enabled or hidden in **Unova → Settings**. Reduced motion is available in launcher settings.

To interact with your lead Pokémon, face it and press **R + A**, or tap its sprite. Talk, pet, play, praise, tell a story, and choose other friendly actions. Reactions are cosmetic and do not affect friendship or battle stats.
