# Features

Unova Together **2.0.11** brings friends, Pokémon tools, and Mega Evolution to supported English Blaze Black 2 Redux profiles on Windows.

## Play with friends

- Explore together and see when friends are nearby or in battle.
- Offer eligible items or party Pokémon from **Unova → Players**. Both players confirm before a trade completes.
- Invite a friend to supported co-op trainer battles or choose a friendly player battle.
- Find practice trainers for battles that do not change story progress.

Both players should use the same release and matching game version. Co-op experience is disabled. For setup and connection choices, see [Playing with friends](PLAYER_GUIDE.md). Some slow or restricted networks may interrupt a session; the [known limits](KNOWN_ISSUES.md) page has tips.

## Mega Evolution

There are **49 Mega forms**, each paired with its own Mega Stone. Every Poké Mart carries the full selection for **₽50,000** per stone, and both Battle Point counters sell all 49 for **50 BP**. Give the matching stone to a Pokémon, then hold **L + R together** while choosing a move in battle, including double battles. The form lasts until the battle ends. Gym Leaders in the final three gyms and Cynthia in her Route 7 rematch also have Mega-capable Pokémon and matching stones.

Examples: Mega Serperior gains **Adaptability**; Mega Charizard Y gains **Drought**; Mega Salamence uses **Intimidate**; Mega Lucario Z gains **Adaptability**. The full roster, abilities, stones, and base-stat totals are in the [Mega Evolution guide](MEGA_EVOLUTION.md).

Animated battle artwork is available for 46 Mega forms. Pokémon with supplied animations play through the full timed sequence.

After defeating Skyla, meet Cynthia near the Mistralton City and Route 7 transition for a Mega Evolution lesson and a free matching stone. Visit her again farther north on Route 7 to battle her team and Mega Garchomp. The launcher prepares a separate event-ready copy for supported game revisions; see the [Cynthia Route 7 guide](CYNTHIA_ROUTE_7.md).

## Following Pokémon

The first Pokémon in your party follows you along your route. All **649 base species** have follower artwork. Followers hide during battles and full-screen menus. You can show or hide your own or a teammate’s follower in **Unova → Settings**.

Face your follower and press **R + A**, or tap its sprite, to open the companion menu. Talk, pet, play, praise, and choose other lighthearted actions. These interactions are cosmetic and do not change friendship, HP, items, or save data. See [Following Pokémon](FOLLOWING_POKEMON.md).

## Pokémon and field tools

- Check party IVs and EVs.
- Reset a Pokémon’s EVs for ₽50,000.
- Keep the standard bag capacity; Mega Stones fit in existing item entries. See [Bag capacity](BAG_CAPACITY.md).
- Browse ordinary grass, water, and fishing encounters in the loaded game.
- Relearn eligible level-up moves for the Pokémon’s current form.
- Use supported field HMs without taking up a battle move slot. The game’s story requirements still apply.
- Solo battles earn 1.25× experience on supported profiles. Co-op experience remains off.

## Optional Soaring

Soaring is an unfinished, optional preview that connects 69 outdoor areas. Named regional places, route links, and added transition scenery help the map flow between locations, while an ocean backdrop fills open spaces within its borders. Some positions are approximate, connector scenery is not landable, and safe landing spots are limited. Read the [Soaring preview guide](SOARING_PREVIEW.md) before you use it.

## Launcher and presentation

The launcher helps you choose a game, manage save slots and backups, connect with friends, and install verified updates. You can adjust controls, display, camera, audio, music, and reduced motion. Optional battle scenery is built from your own game data and can be disabled at any time.

From launcher settings, choose an animated field character available in your supported game. The selection takes effect the next time you start the game; bike and surf forms keep their original animations. Added in-game menus now use a Generation 5-inspired layout and optional menu sounds.
