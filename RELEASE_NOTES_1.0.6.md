# Unova Together 1.0.6

Full release by mvq1303 / WhatWasMissing, based on melonDS 1.1.

Companion interactions now offer ten choices: Talk, Pet, Play, Praise, Tell a story, Ask about today, Sit together, Show a trick, Tell a joke and Sing together. Each choice has four rotating lines across five friendship bands. The lead Pokémon's nickname, HP, friendship and true Gen 5 nature shape the response; nature is shown through subtle behavior instead of being named.

Reactions add question marks, exclamation marks, hearts, stars, musical notes and ellipses, with brief peeking, swaying, hopping, spinning or running motions. Standing follower sprites with no natural idle movement now bob gently while their feet stay grounded.

Hold **R** and press **A** while facing a still follower to open the menu. A by itself remains available to advance game text, avoiding the dialogue softlock. Tapping or clicking the follower also opens the menu. Interactions are cosmetic and read-only: they do not change friendship, HP, items, or saves.

The focused production-code matrix covers 5,000 dialogue and cue combinations across all 25 nature values, five friendship bands, ten actions and four variations. It also checks all 649 supported species, field eligibility, menu/input gating and safe A-button forwarding. The full Windows emulator build and the packaged standalone launcher's isolated startup self-test passed. No ROM, firmware or save is included.
