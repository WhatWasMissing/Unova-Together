# Unova Together 2.0.5 — Mega-capable trainer battles

The Gym Leaders in the final three gyms now bring Mega-capable Pokémon into battle, and Cynthia can Mega Evolve her Garchomp in her Route 7 rematch. Opponents can still Mega Evolve when their Pokémon moves before yours.

Use the launcher to install the update. Your saves are kept.
