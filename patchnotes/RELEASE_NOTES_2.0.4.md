# Unova Together 2.0.4 — Mega Evolution in double battles

Mega Evolution now works reliably in double battles, even when the other Pokémon on your side chooses its move afterward.

This update also makes the launcher’s old-version cleanup easier to follow with a progress indicator, estimated time, and the locations of files being removed. In-game menus show clearer section labels and selection highlights, and you can choose a supported player character before starting the game.

Use the launcher to install the update. Your saves are kept.
