# Unova Together 1.0.20 — Mega battle crash fix

- Fixed the crash that could happen after choosing a move while Mega Evolving Serperior.
- Mega Serperior now keeps its form and stats through the battle, then returns to normal when the battle ends.
- To try it, use Black 2 Redux Complete v1.4.1, give Serperior Serperiorite, open Fight, and press L + R while choosing a move. The Multiplayer Debug tab can grant the stone.
- Mega Serperior is the only Mega in this build. Save normally before testing; live two-player Mega battles still need a test.
