# Unova Together 1.0.29 — Smoother followers and Mega animations

- Pokémon followers stay about one tile behind and move smoothly around corners.
- Launcher menus now have navigation, confirm, and cancel sounds. Change the volume or turn them off in Settings.
- Mega Pokémon with supplied animated artwork play the full animation during battle. Mega Salamence has 67 frames on each side.

Please test Mega Salamence in battle: give it Salamencite and press **L + R together** while choosing a move. Check whether it animates and stays correctly positioned.
