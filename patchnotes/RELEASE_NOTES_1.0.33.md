# Unova Together 1.0.33 — Mega Pokémon and a Soaring preview

- Try 49 Mega Pokémon with their matching Mega Stones. For example, Mega Serperior gains Adaptability and Mega Charizard Y gains Drought.
- Animated battle artwork now plays full timed sequences for 46 Mega forms. Mega Salamence uses 67 frames for both its front and back view.
- Follower walking received a smoothing adjustment. Version 1.0.34 further aligns the visible artwork between poses to address vertical shifting.
- Take flight across the unfinished Soaring preview, which connects 69 outdoor areas.

**Soaring is still unfinished.** Some routes and locations are approximate, live playtesting is still needed, and Marine Tube has no safe landing spot. Save normally before trying it. See the [Mega guide](../docs/MEGA_EVOLUTION.md), [Soaring guide](../docs/SOARING_FLIGHT.md), and [known limits](../docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md) for details.

Update everyone in your co-op group to version 1.0.33 before playing together.
