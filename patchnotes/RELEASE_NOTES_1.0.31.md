# Unova Together 1.0.31 — Smoother followers and landings

- Followers now stay a little farther behind and keep a smooth walking rhythm around turns.
- Soaring landings now return through Pokémon Black 2's Fly-style transition.
- Mega Salamence's full battle animation is ready for a live test.

Walk and turn with a follower to check its spacing and movement. To test Mega Salamence, give it Salamencite and hold **L + R** while choosing a move in battle. To test Soaring, enable **Settings → Experimental features → Soaring flight**, choose **Unova → Pokémon → Take flight**, then land on clear ground and check that you return to the right map and spot.
