# Unova Together 1.0.31 — Smoother Soaring landings

- Soaring landings now use Pokémon Black 2's Fly-style map transition.
- Followers keep a one-tile trail and move smoothly through corners.

Try flight from **Unova → Pokémon → Take flight** with **Settings → Experimental features → Soaring flight** enabled. Land on clear ground and check that you return to the right map and spot. To test Mega Salamence's battle animation, give it Salamencite and hold **L + R** while choosing a move.
