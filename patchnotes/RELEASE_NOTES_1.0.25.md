# Unova Together 1.0.25 — More Mega Pokémon

- Added Mega forms for Ampharos, Venusaur, Charizard Y, Gengar, Alakazam, Mawile, and Garchomp.
- All 19 matching Mega Stones are now sold at Pokémon Center Marts for ₽50,000 each.
- Give a Pokémon its matching stone, then press **L + R together** while choosing a move in battle. It returns to normal when the battle ends.

Save normally before testing. Mega Evolution remains experimental, especially in linked two-player battles.
