# Unova Together 2.0.0 — Mega Evolution and a fresh look

- Explore with **49 Mega forms**, each with a matching Mega Stone sold at Pokémon Center Marts. Forty-six forms have animated battle artwork.
- The launcher’s Features page now plays the supplied front and back animations for Mega Mawile.
- Mega Evolution works in battle, keeps the Mega form through switching and level gains, and returns the Pokémon to normal when the battle ends.
- The launcher now lets you choose an animated field character from your game. Your choice applies the next time you start the game.
- After defeating Skyla, meet Cynthia on Route 7 to learn about Mega Evolution and receive a matching Mega Stone. Return for a rematch against her team and Mega Garchomp. The launcher makes a separate event-ready copy of supported Redux games.
- Added in-game mod menus have a Generation 5-inspired design, with menu sounds you can turn off in **Unova → Settings**.
- The launcher’s old-version cleanup keeps your active game and saves while removing unused copies.

Soaring remains an optional, unfinished preview. Read the [Soaring guide](../docs/SOARING_FLIGHT.md) before using it.
