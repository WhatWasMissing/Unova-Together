# Unova Together 2.0.12 — Soaring flight preview

Soaring flight is now available in the game when enabled in launcher settings. Update to 2.0.12, restart the game, then open **Unova → Pokémon → Take flight (experimental)** and choose an eligible Pokémon.

Soaring remains an unfinished preview. Some areas and landing spots are unsupported or approximate. Your ROM selection and saves are kept when you update.
