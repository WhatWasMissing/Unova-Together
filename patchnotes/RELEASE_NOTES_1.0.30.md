# Unova Together 1.0.30 — Fly between more areas

- Experimental Soaring flight now reaches more connected outdoor routes and towns.
- Followers keep a little more room behind you, and their walking steps stay in time around corners.
- Mega battle animations play the supplied artwork frame by frame.

Try Soaring from **Settings → Experimental features → Soaring flight**, then **Unova → Pokémon → Take flight**. Please check that you can cross between areas and land in the right place. To test Mega Salamence, give it Salamencite and press **L + R** while choosing a move in battle.
