# Game artwork credits

Generation 5 Pokémon sprites belong to Nintendo, Creatures, and GAME FREAK. They are supplied through [PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites), in generation-v/black-white.

Gameplay captures and music were recorded from a Black 2 Redux game file supplied by the player. The original artwork and soundtrack remain the property of their respective owners. Unova Together is an unofficial fan mod by mvq1303 / WhatWasMissing. No generated artwork or music is included.
