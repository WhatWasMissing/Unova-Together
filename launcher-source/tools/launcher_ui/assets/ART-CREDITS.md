## Launcher artwork

### Fan-made Generation 5-style Mega sprites

- `features/gen5-fan/mega-ampharos-front.gif` and `mega-ampharos-back.gif`: animated by **Ghasty_001** in a Generation 5 battle-sprite style. The base sprites are credited by the artist to **Branflakes325, Layell, and Wyverii** from Smogon's Sprite Project. Ghasty_001 states the animations are free to use in other projects with credit and that the base sprites were shared with free-to-use consent. Source: [artist's repository](https://github.com/Ghasty001/Animated_sprites_by_Ghasty001), pinned at commit `25ab1b072dd2fdf96f5c85d503403317f33a857f`; [Eevee Expo resource page](https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1774/).
- `features/gen5-fan/mega-altaria-front.gif` and `mega-altaria-back.gif`: animated by **Ghasty_001** in a Generation 5 battle-sprite style, using base sprites credited to **Falgaia**. The artist's repository states these animations are free to use in other projects with credit and that credited base sprites were shared with free-to-use consent. Source: [artist's repository](https://github.com/Ghasty001/Animated_sprites_by_Ghasty001), pinned at commit `25ab1b072dd2fdf96f5c85d503403317f33a857f`; [Eevee Expo resource page](https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1774/).
- These permissions cover the listed artwork contributions and attribution. They do not grant rights to Pokémon character designs or other franchise material.

### Owner-supplied feature art

- Mega Mawile front and back animations in the Features page: supplied by the project owner from their local Mega artwork collection and included with the owner's permission. The supplied files did not identify the original artist.
