# Unova Together 1.0.5

Full release by mvq1303 / WhatWasMissing, based on melonDS 1.1.

Solo battle XP is now **1.25×**, reduced from 1.65×, for supported English Black 2 and Redux profiles. The launcher progression calculator uses the same multiplier. Already earned XP, saves and level thresholds are unchanged. Co-op XP suppression remains unchanged.

Fractional awards round down and the native 100,000 XP award cap remains. At this rate, solo XP can compensate for approximately 20% of the original XP budget being missed in co-op. Actual levels depend on battle participation.

Compiled production-dispatch checks passed against genuine Redux and English Black 2 overlays, including rounding, award limits, unchanged EVs/flags, co-op zero rewards and invalid-context rejection. These are dispatch checks; a new end-to-end battle was not performed for this release.

Includes the launcher, modified emulator, matching GPL source and checksums. No ROMs, firmware or saves are distributed. The experimental HeartGold port is not enabled in this release. Restart the game to apply the new multiplier.
