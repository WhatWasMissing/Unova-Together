# Unova Together beta 0.3.11 — Field tools

- **Encounter finder:** browse the current area's encounters by season in **Players → Game**.
- **Relearn moves:** choose a level-up move and its replacement slot, then confirm. Relearning is free.
- **Field HMs:** keep the HM in your bag and a non-egg Pokémon in your party. You can use it without occupying a learned move slot. Interact with obstacles normally; choose Fly from the Pokémon menu.

Supports the reviewed English Black 2 routines, including Redux Complete v1.4.1. Original story requirements still apply. If all four of a Pokémon's moves already have field actions, choose another Pokémon for Fly.

Download **Unova-Together.exe**, or extract the Windows ZIP and run **Start Unova Together.cmd**. Existing users can use **Updates** in the launcher. Restart the emulator after updating; no new ROM or save is required.

Encounter tables, learnsets, move replacement and all six HM eligibility paths passed focused checks. Native menus passed on a copied save, and Fly opened the original destination menu with JIT enabled. Full HM traversal, completed Fly travel and save/reload after relearning have not been playtested. Back up your save before trying these beta features.

The encounter finder excludes gifts, scripted legendary encounters and Hidden Grottos. Relearning offers the current species/form's level-up moves at or below its current level. See [field tools](https://github.com/WhatWasMissing/Unova-Together/blob/main/docs/FIELD_TOOLS.md) and [known issues](https://github.com/WhatWasMissing/Unova-Together/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
